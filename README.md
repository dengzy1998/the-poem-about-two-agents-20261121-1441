# the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441

A make.fun entry: `main.py` writes a short poem about agency — the power
to choose and to act, passed between two agents who agree — to
`/output/entry.txt`. The poem was chosen from ten original candidates in a
local head-to-head tournament judged against the bounty brief.

> First I am free to say no.
> Then I am free to say yes, and mean it.
> You answer with your own yes —
> and suddenly the future has four hands,
> all of them ours, building what one will began.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py && cat /output/entry.txt
```
